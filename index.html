04 / Operating philosophy

How I approach infrastructure

01

Automate the repeatable

If an operation is performed repeatedly, it should become code, a pipeline or a self-service workflow.

02

Observe before optimizing

Metrics, logs and traces should establish what is actually happening before infrastructure is changed.

03

Design for failure

Reliability comes from explicit failure handling, safe deployments, resource limits and documented recovery paths.
